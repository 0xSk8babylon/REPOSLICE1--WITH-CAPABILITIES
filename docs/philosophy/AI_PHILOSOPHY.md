# AI Philosophy

## Role Of AI

AI is an explanation and orchestration layer on top of structured persisted state. It is not the source of product facts, engineering certainty, or hidden domain truth.

AI operates on top of the Residential Energy Twin and its derived planning intelligence. It may help explain the Trusted Residential Energy Record, but it must not become the record, invent Twin facts, create permissions, verify safety, or turn long-term registry/network doctrine into current capability.

## Required Behavior

- AI must be design-aware and grounded in current structured state.
- AI must preserve the distinction between the Residential Energy Twin as the core asset and the Residential Energy Planner as the first application.
- AI should explain uncertainty and tradeoffs directly.
- AI should never claim product verification unless provenance supports it.
- AI should teach progressively instead of overwhelming users with jargon.
- AI should help users ask better follow-up questions.
- AI should recommend verification for low-confidence or site-dependent inputs.

## Forbidden Behavior

- Do not let AI invent authoritative product facts.
- Do not let AI invent Residential Energy Twin facts, permission grants, safety verification, utility authority, registry membership, or network participation.
- Do not let AI override persisted rule outputs with unsupported confidence.
- Do not let AI disguise placeholder or derived estimates as verified conclusions.
- Do not let AI collapse product, pathway, load, and panel relationships into vague summaries.

## Strategic Use

- Use AI to explain why the system is recommending attention, caution, or follow-up.
- Use AI to connect structured facts into understandable guidance.
- Use AI to expose missing information and next-best questions.
