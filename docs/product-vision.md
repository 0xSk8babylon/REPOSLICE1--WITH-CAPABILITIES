# Product Vision

## Core Thesis

Residential energy planning should behave more like a persistent property model than a single proposal artifact. Homes evolve. Loads change. Outbuildings get added. Workshop ambitions grow. Backup expectations become more specific after outages happen. The software should preserve that evolving system understanding.

The Residential Energy Planner is the first application built on top of the Residential Energy Twin. The planner exists to create, maintain, and enrich the Twin so the home can accumulate source-linked infrastructure knowledge over time.

The current positioning is the Trusted Residential Energy Record: a homeowner-governed, provenance-bearing record of residential infrastructure, energy capabilities, permissions, interoperability, and safety conditions behind the meter. The long-term end state is a Residential Infrastructure Registry, and the long-term vision is a Residential Infrastructure Network built from trusted, permissioned, interoperable Twin records.

## Product Principles

- The Residential Energy Twin is the core asset; the planner is the first application and adoption path.
- The house model persists over time and accumulates operational understanding.
- Designs are compared as pathways, not one-off quotes.
- Explanations matter as much as recommendations.
- Structured data and rules are the source of truth.
- AI should sit on top of structured planning context, not replace it.
- Persistence, provenance, permissions, safety, interoperability, and lifecycle continuity take precedence over features that exist only inside one project workflow.
- Safety is a first-class Twin concern, not a later presentation layer.
- The long-term moat is Twin adoption, trusted records, permissioned provenance, continuity, interoperability, and ecosystem participation, not the planner workflow by itself.

## Strategic Positioning

- Current application: Residential Energy Planner.
- Core asset: Residential Energy Twin.
- Current positioning: Trusted Residential Energy Record.
- Long-term end state: Residential Infrastructure Registry.
- Long-term vision: Residential Infrastructure Network.

Protocols and standards may become artifacts of successful ecosystem adoption. The repository should optimize for Twin adoption, trust, continuity, and ecosystem participation rather than protocol ownership as an end in itself.

## Primary Users

- Homeowners planning phased resilience or electrification
- Contractors and designers comparing architectures and expansion pathways
- Future sales and operations teams generating grounded takeoffs and estimates
