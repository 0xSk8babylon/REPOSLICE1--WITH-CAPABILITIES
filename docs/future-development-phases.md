# Future Development Phases

## Phase 1

- persistent CRUD for homes, buildings, panels, loads, designs, and products
- verified product ingestion workflows
- first deterministic compatibility and sizing rules

## Phase 2

- richer site modeling and equipment location mapping
- scenario scoring with transparent methods
- takeoff generation tied to product mappings and design topology

## Phase 3

- conversational design advisor grounded in structured context
- estimate workflows with assumption tracking
- customer-facing collaboration history and change diffs

## Explicitly Deferred

- full NEC compliance automation
- permitting and AHJ submission workflows
- production-grade auth, billing, and deployment hardening
