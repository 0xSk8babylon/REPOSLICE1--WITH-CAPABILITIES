# ADR 0006: Pathways And Products Both Matter

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Energy planning decisions are shaped by both product choice and infrastructure routing. Treating products as sufficient without pathways hides future cost, complexity, and upgrade consequences.

## Decision

The platform should model products and pathways as distinct but interdependent planning concerns.

## Consequences

- Scenario comparison should consider both product signals and pathway/infrastructure signals.
- UX should expose routing, siting, expansion, and visibility implications alongside product choices.
- Future sessions should avoid collapsing infrastructure strategy into a simple equipment catalog.
